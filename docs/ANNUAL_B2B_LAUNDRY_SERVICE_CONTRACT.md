## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Clean Clear shall perform institutional and corporate laundry services for Client, categorized into:
1. **Linen & Bulk Weight Wash**: Bulk sheets, duvet covers, bath towels, table cloths, and kitchen linens billed on a per-kilogram (₹/Kg) weight basis.
2. **Uniforms & Piece-Rate Care**: Staff suits, blazers, chef jackets, shirts, trousers, and aprons processed individually with stain pretreatment and industrial steam finishing.
3. **Executive Dry Cleaning**: Specialized solvent-based dry cleaning for delicate garments, woolens, silks, and heavy upholstery fabrics.
4. **Packaging & Tagging**: Every processed batch shall be heat-sealed in protective polythene packaging, itemized, and accompanied by a digital POS delivery challan / invoice.

## 4. INTAKE VERIFICATION, SORTING & COUNT AUDIT

4.1. At the time of pickup, both Parties’ representatives shall sign a physical or digital intake counter slip detailing the total bag count and piece count.  
4.2. **Sorting & Verification**: Within three (3) hours of arrival at the Clean Clear processing facility:
- All garments shall be sorted, weighed (for weight-based billing), and inspected for pre-existing defects (tears, broken buttons, color bleeding).
- A digital intake notification with itemized line items shall be transmitted to the Client’s designated WhatsApp or email contact.
- Discrepancies between the intake slip and actual count must be reported to the Client within four (4) hours of sorting.

---

## 5. QUALITY ASSURANCE, LOSS & DAMAGE POLICY

5.1. **Standard of Care**: Clean Clear warrants that all items shall be washed, treated, and finished according to international fabric care guidelines using eco-safe commercial detergents, softeners, and approved dry-cleaning solvents.  
5.2. **Complimentary Re-cleaning**: Any garment with persistent removable stains or unsatisfactory pressing shall be re-cleaned free of charge if notified within twenty-four (24) hours of delivery.  
5.3. **Maximum Liability for Loss or Damage**:
- In the rare event of irreversible garment damage or total loss attributable directly to Clean Clear’s operational negligence, compensation shall be assessed based on the depreciated market value of the item, capped at a maximum of **ten (10) times the cleaning service charge** for that specific item, or up to **₹ 3,000 per garment**, whichever is lower.
- For high-value designer uniforms or luxury linen exceeding ₹ 10,000 in replacement value, Client must declare the valuation in writing prior to intake.  
5.4. **Unclaimed Goods Clause**: Pursuant to store policies, completed orders not received or accepted by Client within thirty (30) days of promised delivery notice shall not remain under Clean Clear’s custodial liability.

---

## 6. PRICING, INVOICING & PAYMENT TERMS

6.1. **Corporate Rate Card**: Services shall be invoiced based on the discounted Corporate / B2B Price Matrix attached as **Schedule A** (reflecting preferred corporate discounts of 15% to 25% below retail counter rates).  
6.2. **Taxes**: All billing is subject to **Goods and Services Tax (GST) at 18%** (CGST 9% + SGST 9%), itemized on official tax invoices.  
6.3. **Billing Cycles & Payment Options**:
- **Option 1: Prepaid Corporate Wallet (Recommended)**  
  Client deposits an advance corporate package balance of **₹ 25,000 / ₹ 50,000** into the Clean Clear digital wallet. Each pickup order total is automatically deducted upon intake. An extra **10% bonus credit** is credited to the Client upon each wallet recharge.
- **Option 2: Monthly Consolidated Invoicing (Credit Account)**  
  Clean Clear shall generate a consolidated monthly tax invoice on the 1st day of each calendar month covering all orders processed in the preceding month. Client shall remit payment via NEFT/RTGS/UPI within **fifteen (15) calendar days (Net-15)** of invoice receipt.
6.4. **Late Payment Fee**: Overdue payments past Net-15 shall incur late interest of 1.5% per month. Service may be paused if outstanding overdue balance exceeds thirty (30) days.

## 7. TERMINATION

7.1. **Termination for Convenience**: Either Party may terminate this Agreement prior to the expiration of the 1-year term by providing **thirty (30) calendar days prior written notice**.  
7.2. **Termination for Material Breach**: Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party fails to cure a material breach (such as repeated non-payment or persistent severe quality failure) within fourteen (14) days of written notice.  
7.3. **Account Settlement**: Upon termination, all unbilled processed laundry shall be delivered, and all outstanding amounts shall become payable within seven (7) business days. Any unused prepaid wallet balance shall be refunded to Client within seven (7) business days after deducting pending dues.
