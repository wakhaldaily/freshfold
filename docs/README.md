## 1. Annual Software Maintenance & SLA Agreement Overview

- **Scope**: Maintenance, feature updates, cloud infrastructure operations (Cloudflare Workers API, Neon PostgreSQL DB, Vite React frontend), thermal receipt engine, WhatsApp bill dispatch, data backups, and security patching.
- **Duration**: Exactly **12 Months (1 Year)** from effective date with automatic renewal review.
- **Service Level Agreement (SLA)**:
  - **99.9% Target Cloud Availability**.
  - **Severity 1 (Critical Outage / POS Blocked)**: Response within 1 hour; resolution target within 4 hours.
  - **Severity 2 (Major Feature Impaired)**: Response within 4 hours; resolution target within 24 hours.
  - **Severity 3 (Minor Defect / Cosmetic)**: Response within 24 hours; scheduled release cycle.
- **Security & Data**: Encrypted bill URLs, role-based pin/credential guards, daily automated Neon DB snapshots, and complete end-of-term data export guarantee.

## 2. Annual B2B Laundry Services Agreement Overview

- **Scope**: Commercial dry cleaning, wash & fold, steam pressing, and linen care for institutional/corporate clients under the `Corporate / B2B Contract` rate tier.
- **Duration**: Exactly **12 Months (1 Year)**.
- **Operational Cadence**: Scheduled weekly pickups and deliveries, express/same-day surge clauses, weight vs. piece piece counts, itemized tagging, and stain handling protocol.
- **Billing & Settlement**: Monthly Net-15/30 invoicing, 18% GST compliance, wallet package advance credits, and loss/damage compensation limits aligned with retail store policy.

## How to Execute These Contracts

1. **Review and Fill Placeholders**: Search for bracketed fields such as `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`, `[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]`, `[CONSULTING FEE]`, or `[AUTHORIZED SIGNATORY]`.
2. **Review Annexures / Schedules**:
   - For the Software Contract: Verify the payment milestone schedules and hosting allowance.
   - For the B2B Contract: Verify the negotiated corporate garment/weight price list in Schedule A.
3. **Execution**: Print two copies on corporate letterhead / e-sign via DocuSign or Aadhaar e-Sign / Stamp paper as per applicable jurisdictional requirements.
4. **Archival**: Retain countersigned digital copies within your secure document repository and link active contract codes in the customer POS directory (`CUST-XXXX`).
